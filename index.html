KaPnTV

A home screen for Android TV and Google TV.

What it is

KaPnTV replaces your TV's home screen with a fast, customisable launcher: a floating dock of your apps, previews of what each app is offering, and rows of everything else below. It runs entirely on your TV. There is no KaPnTV account and no KaPnTV server.

Features

  • A dock of your favourite apps, with Continue watching and recommendations above each one
  • Rows of apps and content below the dock, which you can rename, reorder and edit
  • Recent apps, quick settings, HDMI inputs, weather, network and Bluetooth status
  • Themes, picture and video backgrounds, icon packs, and a PIN lock
  • Optional: connect YouTube to see Latest from your subscriptions and Liked videos

YouTube (optional)

If you choose to connect YouTube in Settings ▸ Accounts, KaPnTV asks Google for read-only access to your YouTube account. It uses that access only to show your subscriptions' latest videos and your liked videos on your TV, and to open them in the YouTube app. Your data stays on your TV and is never sold or shared. You can disconnect at any time, which also removes KaPnTV's access from your Google account. Read the privacy policy for details.
